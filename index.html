What matters

They version their hooks, and the file name proves it

The Wistia asset is titled “4K USB HOOK v3” — visible in the play button's accessible label. That is an internal name that leaked into production markup. It tells you the hook is a tracked, versioned artefact on at least its third iteration, and that the 4K/USB naming is their shorthand for a hook family. We do not version our VSL hooks or name them. They do, and it shows in the markup.

The date in the scarcity line is injected live

The CTA read “Be Quick! As of Today 18th Of August, All Spots For This Month Are Almost Gone!” on the day of capture. It is evergreen scarcity that never goes stale and never needs a human to update it. Costs one line of JS.

The free call is given a price before it is given away

The quiz button says GIVE ME THE $1,000 STRATEGY FOR FREE. The call is anchored at $1,000 in the same breath it is made free, so the prospect books something valued rather than something cheap. Our class is “free” with no number attached to it.

The training is delivered by SMS, so the phone number is the real opt-in

“GET ACCESS NOW! CHECK YA PHONE AS WE'LL TEXT YOU THE TRAINING IMMEDIATELY”. The deliverable is the reason to give a mobile number, and it makes the first text an expected one. Same problem Suprahuman and PB Trading solve differently.
